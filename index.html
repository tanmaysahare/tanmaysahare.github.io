CORE Capital Optimization & Risk Engine

The Problem

Retail investors in India are mostly naked-long, and over 90% of F&O traders lose money. They lack affordable, automated risk defense.

The Solution

CORE acts as an automated "Chief Risk Officer"—stress-testing portfolios against crashes and prescribing hedges in 3 clicks.

  • AI Risk Engine: VADER-based sentiment scanner adjusts hedge ratios live.
  • Crisis Replay: Simulates 2008 & 2020 crashes on user portfolios.
  • Zero-Cost Collar: Downside protection with zero net premium.
  • Portfolio DNA: Correlation & sector-concentration analysis.
  • Tax Scout: Algorithmic tax-loss harvesting execution.
